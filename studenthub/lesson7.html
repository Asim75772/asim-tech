Lesson 7: The Rebel Poet

The Rebel Poet

← Class 5← Previous

Notes

  • The lesson introduces the spirit of rebellion against injustice.
  • The poet's writing expresses courage and freedom.
  • A rebel may speak against unfairness.
  • Literature can inspire people to think and act bravely.

Examples

1. Courage is needed to speak against injustice.
2. Freedom is a major idea in rebellious writing.
3. A poet can use words to express strong ideas.

Practice

1. What does 'rebel' mean?
2. Why is courage important?
3. Write one sentence about freedom.
4. What can poetry express?
5. What is injustice?
