📘 Notes

1. What is Geometry?

Geometry is the branch of mathematics that helps us study shapes, lines, angles, sides and positions.

2. Basic Shapes

  • Triangle: 3 sides and 3 corners.
  • Square: 4 equal sides and 4 corners.
  • Rectangle: 4 sides; opposite sides are equal.
  • Circle: a round shape with no sides or corners.

3. Line, Line Segment and Ray

  • Line: extends endlessly in both directions.
  • Line segment: has two fixed endpoints.
  • Ray: has one endpoint and extends endlessly in one direction.

4. Angles

An angle is formed when two lines or rays meet at a point called the vertex.

  • Right angle: exactly 90°
  • Acute angle: less than 90°
  • Obtuse angle: more than 90° but less than 180°
  • Straight angle: exactly 180°
Remember: A right angle = 90°

5. Perimeter

The perimeter is the total distance around a closed shape.

Rectangle: Perimeter = 2 × (Length + Breadth)
Square: Perimeter = 4 × Side

6. Symmetry

A shape is symmetrical when one half matches the other half after folding along a line called the line of symmetry.

✏️ Solved Examples

Example 1: How many sides does a triangle have?
Answer: 3 sides.
Example 2: Find the perimeter of a square with side 6 cm.
Solution: 4 × 6 = 24 cm.
Example 3: Find the perimeter of a rectangle of length 8 cm and breadth 5 cm.
Solution: 2 × (8 + 5) = 2 × 13 = 26 cm.
Example 4: What type of angle is 90°?
Answer: A right angle.
Example 5: Is a square symmetrical?
Answer: Yes. A square has lines of symmetry.
Example 6: How many endpoints does a line segment have?
Answer: 2 endpoints.
