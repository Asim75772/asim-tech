India: Superpower in Cricket

Notes

  • India won its first Cricket World Cup in 1983.
  • The lesson describes India's growth in cricket.
  • The 1983 final was played against West Indies.
  • Krishnamachari Srikkanth was India's highest scorer in the final.

Examples

1. India's 1983 World Cup victory became a memorable moment.
2. West Indies won the toss in the 1983 final.
3. India scored 183 runs in the final.

Practice

1. In which year did India win its first World Cup?
2. Who was India's highest scorer in the 1983 final?
3. Which team was the opponent in the final?
4. How many runs did India score?
5. Why is 1983 important in Indian cricket?
