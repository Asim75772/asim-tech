📘 Notes

1. Decimal Place Value

Digits after the decimal point show parts of a whole.

4.582 = 4 ones + 5 tenths + 8 hundredths + 2 thousandths

2. Tenths, Hundredths and Thousandths

  • 0.1 = one tenth
  • 0.01 = one hundredth
  • 0.001 = one thousandth

3. Writing Decimals

Zeroes can be added to the right of a decimal without changing its value.

0.7 = 0.70 = 0.700

4. Comparing Decimals

First compare the whole-number part. If it is equal, compare tenths, then hundredths, then thousandths.

3.45 > 3.405 because 3.450 > 3.405

5. Adding Decimals

Write the numbers one below another with decimal points aligned.

12.35 + 4.60 = 16.95

6. Subtracting Decimals

Align decimal points and subtract from right to left. Add zeroes when useful.

8.50 − 3.25 = 5.25

7. Multiplying a Decimal by 10 or 100

When multiplying by 10, the decimal point moves one place to the right. By 100, it moves two places to the right.

2.35 × 10 = 23.5    2.35 × 100 = 235

8. Decimals in Daily Life

Decimals are used for money, measurements, distance, weight and temperature.

₹25.50 = 25 rupees and 50 paise
