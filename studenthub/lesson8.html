Lesson 8: Buildings to Remember

Buildings to Remember

← Class 5← Previous

Notes

  • The lesson focuses on important buildings and their historical value.
  • Architecture can tell us about the past.
  • Public buildings and monuments may have cultural importance.
  • We should respect and conserve historic structures.

Examples

1. A historic building is valuable because of its connection with the past.
2. Architecture includes the design and construction of buildings.
3. Conservation means protecting something from damage.

Practice

1. Buildings to Remember পাঠে buildings কেন গুরুত্বপূর্ণ?

5. পাঠের মূল শিক্ষা কী?
