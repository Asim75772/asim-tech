Lesson 11: The Finishing Point

The Finishing Point

← Class 5← Previous

Notes

  • The lesson highlights effort, competition and reaching a goal.
  • The finishing point marks the end of a race or task.
  • Success requires persistence and focus.
  • Fair play and respect are important in competition.

Examples

1. A runner keeps going until the finishing point.
2. Persistence means continuing despite difficulty.
3. Winning is important, but fair play is also important.

Practice

1. What does “finishing point” mean in the lesson?
2. What is needed to complete a task?
3. What is important on the way to reaching a goal?
4. When is it right to stop trying?
5. What is the lesson of the chapter?
