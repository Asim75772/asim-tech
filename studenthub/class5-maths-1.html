📘 Notes

1. Place Value

The value of a digit depends on its position in a number.

In 45,672: 4 = 40,000; 5 = 5,000; 6 = 600; 7 = 70; 2 = 2.

2. Face Value

Face value is the digit itself. The face value of 7 in 47,215 is 7.

3. Expanded Form

56,304 = 50,000 + 6,000 + 300 + 4

4. Comparing Numbers

Compare digits from the highest place value. The first larger digit gives the larger number.

5. Ascending and Descending Order

Ascending means smallest to largest. Descending means largest to smallest.

6. Rounding

If the next digit is 5 or more, increase the rounding digit by 1. If it is less than 5, keep it unchanged.

7. Addition and Subtraction

Arrange numbers according to place value and calculate from right to left.
