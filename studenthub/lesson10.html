Lesson 10: A Great Social Reformer

A Great Social Reformer

← Class 5← Previous

Notes

  • The lesson presents the work and ideas of a social reformer.
  • A social reformer works to improve society.
  • Reform may challenge harmful customs and inequality.
  • Education and awareness can bring positive social change.

Examples

1. A reformer wants society to become better.
2. Social change often needs courage and persistence.
3. Education can help people question unfair practices.

Practice

1. What is a social reformer?
2. Why is education important for social change?
3. Write one quality of a reformer.
4. What is inequality?
5. Write two ways to improve society.
