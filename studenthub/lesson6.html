Lesson 6: The Clever Monkey

The Clever Monkey

← Class 5← Previous

Notes

  • The story highlights intelligence and quick thinking.
  • The monkey uses its presence of mind to face danger.
  • Cleverness can help solve difficult situations.
  • The story teaches us to think before acting.

Examples

1. A clever person looks for a safe solution.
2. Presence of mind means thinking quickly in a difficult situation.
3. We should not act in panic.

Practice

1. Who is the clever character?
2. What does cleverness mean?
3. Write the moral of the story.
4. Make a sentence with 'danger'.
5. Why should we think before acting?
