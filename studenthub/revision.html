Notes

  • Reading a short passage
  • Using simple nouns and verbs
  • Making complete sentences

Examples

1. A learner reads, understands and answers questions.
2. A noun names a person, place, animal or thing.
3. A verb tells an action.

Practice

1. 1. Make a sentence with 'school'.
2. 2. Underline the verb: Birds fly.
3. 3. Write the plural of child.
4. 4. Write one sentence about your family.
5. 5. Write the opposite of happy.

MCQ Test

1. Which word is a noun?
A) run
B) school
C) quickly
D) beautiful
AnswerB
2. Which is a verb?
A) book
B) blue
C) write
D) class
AnswerC
3. Plural of 'child' is
A) childs
B) childes
C) children
D) childrens
AnswerC
4. Opposite of 'early' is
A) late
B) fast
C) soon
D) first
AnswerA
5. A sentence begins with a
A) small letter
B) capital letter
C) number
D) comma
AnswerB
