📘 Notes

1. What is Data?

Data means a collection of information. For example, the favourite fruits of students can be collected as data.

2. Tally Marks

Tally marks are a quick way to count things. Every group of five is represented by four vertical marks crossed by a fifth mark.

3. Frequency

Frequency tells us how many times a particular item occurs.

4. Pictograph

A pictograph represents data using pictures or symbols. A key tells us how many objects each picture represents.

Example: ⭐ = 2 students

5. Bar Graph

A bar graph represents data using bars. The height or length of each bar shows the quantity.

6. Reading a Graph

To read a graph, first look at the title, then check the labels and scale. Finally compare the bars or symbols.

7. Maximum and Minimum

  • Maximum: the greatest value.
  • Minimum: the smallest value.

✏️ Solved Examples

Example 1: A class has 12 boys and 15 girls. How many students are there?
Solution: 12 + 15 = 27 students.
Example 2: In a survey, Apple = 8, Mango = 6, Banana = 5 and Orange = 4. Which fruit is most popular?
Answer: Apple, with 8 students.
Example 3: Using the same data, which fruit is least popular?
Answer: Orange, with 4 students.
Example 4: If ⭐ = 2 students, how many students are represented by ⭐⭐⭐⭐?
Solution: 4 × 2 = 8 students.
Example 5: A student reads 3 books in January and 5 books in February. How many books altogether?
Solution: 3 + 5 = 8 books.
Example 6: The number of trees planted by four groups is 10, 15, 12 and 8. Which group planted the most?
Answer: The group that planted 15 trees.
