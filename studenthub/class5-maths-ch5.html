📘 Notes

1. Measurement

Measurement tells us the size, length, weight or capacity of an object using standard units.

2. Length

  • 10 millimetres (mm) = 1 centimetre (cm)
  • 100 centimetres = 1 metre (m)
  • 1000 metres = 1 kilometre (km)
1 m = 100 cm    |    1 km = 1000 m

3. Mass / Weight

  • 1000 milligrams (mg) = 1 gram (g)
  • 1000 grams = 1 kilogram (kg)
  • 1000 kilograms = 1 tonne (t)
1 kg = 1000 g    |    1 t = 1000 kg

4. Capacity

  • 1000 millilitres (mL) = 1 litre (L)
  • 1 L = 1000 mL

5. Time

  • 60 seconds = 1 minute
  • 60 minutes = 1 hour
  • 24 hours = 1 day
  • 7 days = 1 week

6. Converting Units

To convert a larger unit into a smaller unit, usually multiply. To convert a smaller unit into a larger unit, usually divide.

3 m = 3 × 100 = 300 cm
5000 m = 5000 ÷ 1000 = 5 km

✏️ Solved Examples

Example 1: Convert 4 m into cm.
Solution: 4 × 100 = 400 cm.
Example 2: Convert 3 kg into grams.
Solution: 3 × 1000 = 3000 g.
Example 3: Convert 2500 m into km and m.
Solution: 2500 m = 2 km 500 m.
Example 4: Convert 2 L into mL.
Solution: 2 × 1000 = 2000 mL.
Example 5: How many minutes are there in 3 hours?
Solution: 3 × 60 = 180 minutes.
Example 6: Which is longer: 2 m or 150 cm?
Solution: 2 m = 200 cm, so 2 m is longer.
