Lesson 4: Memory in Marble

Memory in Marble

← Class 5← Previous

Notes

  • The lesson connects marble monuments with history and memory.
  • Historical monuments help us remember important people and events.
  • Buildings can preserve stories of the past.
  • We should protect heritage sites.

Examples

1. A monument can remind future generations about history.
2. Marble is a stone often used in famous monuments.
3. Preserving heritage protects our connection with the past.

Practice

1. What does marble refer to in “Memory in Marble”?
2. What is one way to preserve a memory permanently?
3. Why are memorials important?
4. What is one source for learning about history?
5. What does the lesson teach us to do?
