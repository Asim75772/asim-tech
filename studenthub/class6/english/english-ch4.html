Study Guide

📘 Lesson Overview

Original study notes prepared for StudentHub. They are designed to help students understand and revise the lesson without reproducing the textbook.

💡 Key Learning Points

  • A humorous imaginative story centred on a mysterious shop that seems not to exist.
  • The lesson develops inference: readers must distinguish what characters expect from what actually happens.
  • Notice the role of surprise and misunderstanding.

🔤 Useful Vocabulary

❓ Practice Questions & Answers

1. Why is the title unusual?

It suggests a shop that cannot be found or does not actually exist.

2. How does mystery make a story interesting?

Mystery makes readers curious.

3. Write a sentence using ‘mysterious’.

Answers may vary.
