📘 Lesson Overview

This humorous story centres on Bholenath, a simple potter, and a misunderstanding involving the mysterious sound “drip-drip”. A frightened tiger mistakes the sound for something dangerous, while events later lead Bholenath into an unexpected adventure.

Learning focus: reading comprehension, sequencing events, vocabulary, adjectives, verbs and sentence construction.

💡 Easy Explanation

The story uses a funny misunderstanding to create suspense. A tiger hears a repeated sound and imagines that “Drip-Drip” is a frightening creature. Bholenath, unaware of the tiger’s fear, becomes part of a chain of comic events. The story shows how fear and misunderstanding can make an ordinary situation seem extraordinary.

❓ Short Questions & Answers

1. Who was Bholenath?
Bholenath was a simple potter.
2. Why did the tiger take shelter near the old woman’s hut?
It was seeking a safe place to hide.
3. What did the tiger think “Drip-Drip” was?
The tiger misunderstood the sound and thought it was a dangerous creature.
4. Why is the story humorous?
It is humorous because the characters misunderstand ordinary events, creating unexpected and funny situations.
