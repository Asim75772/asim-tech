Study Guide

📘 Lesson Overview

Original study notes prepared for StudentHub. They are designed to help students understand and revise the lesson without reproducing the textbook.

💡 Key Learning Points

  • Number: child→children, man→men, ox→oxen, goose→geese, deer→deer
  • Gender: masculine, feminine, neuter and common gender
  • Articles: a, an, the
  • Prepositions, adjectives, verb forms, punctuation and nouns
  • Personal and possessive pronouns
