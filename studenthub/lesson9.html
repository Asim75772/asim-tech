Lesson 9: The Bird’s Eye

The Bird's Eye

← Class 5← Previous

Notes

  • The lesson uses the idea of a bird's eye view to look at places from above.
  • A bird flying high can see a wider area.
  • The idea helps us understand observation and perspective.
  • Careful observation gives us more information.

Examples

1. A bird's eye view means seeing something from a high position.
2. From above, roads and fields may look like patterns.
3. Observation means looking carefully to notice details.

Practice
